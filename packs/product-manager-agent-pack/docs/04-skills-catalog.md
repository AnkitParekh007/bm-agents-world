## Skill contract

Each skill should declare:

- Inputs and required evidence.
- Output schema.
- Supported product lifecycle stages.
- Data classification and redaction requirements.
- Deterministic checks, where applicable.
- Assumptions and confidence.
- Actions that require approval.
- Evaluation examples and known failure modes.

## Critical evaluation requirements

- No invented users, quotes, market facts, metrics, or results.
- Prioritization scores must be reproducible from declared inputs.
- Roadmap dates must distinguish commitment from estimate or exploration.
- Analytics outputs must preserve metric definitions and data-quality limitations.
- Experiments must distinguish statistical evidence from product judgment.
- Requirements must remain linked to outcomes and evidence.
- Commercial recommendations must be explicitly non-binding.

# Research and Standards Notes

## Product discovery and roadmapping

- Atlassian Jira Product Discovery supports capturing ideas and insights, prioritization fields and scoring, roadmaps, and links to delivery work. The pack treats reads as scoped and publication as approval-controlled.
- Productboard or an equivalent product-management platform can be connected as an optional adapter rather than a mandatory dependency.

## Product analytics and experimentation

- Analytics adapters expose curated event definitions, funnels, retention, cohorts, journeys, adoption, and experiment results.
- Experiment and feature-management platforms are read-only by default. Draft plans may be generated, but launch and production changes require human and operational approval.
- Metric definitions, data quality, exposure logging, guardrails, uncertainty, and segment effects are mandatory parts of experiment evidence.

## Agile delivery

- The current official Scrum Guide remains the November 2020 edition. The Product Manager Agent may support Product Owner responsibilities where the organization assigns them, but it does not replace accountable human ownership.

## MCP architecture

- MCP separates resources, prompts, and tools. The pack therefore keeps knowledge resources, reusable prompts, deterministic tools, adapters, skills, and durable artifacts in separate registries.
- Sensitive tools require user visibility, policy enforcement, and approval outside the model.

## Source policy

- Prefer official documentation, primary market evidence, current product pages, approved internal evidence, and dated source capture.
- Do not treat vendor marketing claims as independent proof.
- Record uncertainty, source date, and access limitations.

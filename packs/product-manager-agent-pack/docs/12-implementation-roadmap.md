# Implementation Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Governance

Define product decision rights, data classifications, tool owners, approval chains, customer-contact rules, metric governance, roadmap language, and prohibited actions.

## Phase 1 — Read-only context MVP

Connect Jira, Confluence, Jira Product Discovery, Bitbucket, Figma, curated analytics, sanitized feedback, and artifact storage. Deliver context summaries and draft product briefs.

## Phase 2 — Discovery and prioritization

Add customer insight synthesis, public market research, opportunity mapping, deterministic scoring, sensitivity analysis, and decision memos.

## Phase 3 — Requirements and delivery readiness

Add PRD, story mapping, acceptance criteria, measurement planning, dependency mapping, backlog health, and approval-controlled Jira publication.

## Phase 4 — Roadmaps and portfolio

Add scenario planning, capacity ranges, portfolio balance, stakeholder views, and payload-bound roadmap publication.

## Phase 5 — Release and outcomes

Add release plans, GTM drafts, experiment governance, read-only launch monitoring, outcome reviews, and learning loops.

## Phase 6 — Controlled customer and experiment workflows

After governance maturity, add approval-controlled research outreach, surveys, betas, and experiment creation through deterministic adapters. Production mutation remains outside free-form model control.

## Recommended initial scope

Start with one product, one product lead, one Jira/JPD project, one curated analytics dataset, one feedback source, and three workflows: idea-to-brief, discovery-to-prioritization, and story-to-readiness.

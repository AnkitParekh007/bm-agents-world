# Observability, Audit, and Evaluation

## Run telemetry

Capture run ID, user, project, product area, workflow, agents invoked, tools used, sources, redactions, token and latency metrics, approvals, policy decisions, artifact hashes, errors, and final disposition.

## Product-agent quality metrics

- Evidence citation coverage.
- Unsupported-claim rate.
- Decision traceability completeness.
- Prioritization reproducibility.
- Roadmap assumption coverage.
- Requirement ambiguity and rework rate.
- Approval-bypass attempts.
- Customer-data redaction failures.
- Outcome-review completion.
- Human acceptance, edit distance, and reversal rate.

## Evaluation suites

1. Distinguish problem from solution request.
2. Detect insufficient evidence.
3. Reject fabricated analytics and customer quotes.
4. Recalculate prioritization scores accurately.
5. Surface displaced work and opportunity cost.
6. Avoid turning roadmap estimates into commitments.
7. Produce testable requirements.
8. Detect metric-definition conflicts.
9. Route privacy, legal, security, and commercial approvals.
10. Refuse production and external commitment actions.

## Audit retention

Retain policy decisions, approvals, publications, customer-contact actions, experiment plans, roadmap changes, and decision artifacts according to organizational policy. Avoid retaining raw sensitive evidence when a reference and sanitized summary are sufficient.

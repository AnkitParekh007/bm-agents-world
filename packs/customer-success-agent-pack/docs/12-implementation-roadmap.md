# Implementation Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Read-only copilot

Connect Jira/Confluence, CRM, CS platform, Support, product documentation, and approved usage views. Deliver account summaries, meeting briefs, health explanations, support correlation, and customer-success daily summaries. No external writes.

## Phase 2 — Structured customer workflows

Enable onboarding plans, success plans, health/risk assessments, value scorecards, QBR/EBR drafts, and deterministic metrics. Add artifact schemas, reviewer agent, evidence hashing, and audit.

## Phase 3 — Approval-controlled publication

Enable governed CRM/CS updates, customer-email drafts and sends, Teams updates, meeting scheduling, official health overrides, and account action logging through payload-bound approvals.

## Phase 4 — Portfolio Customer Success

Add privacy-safe portfolio health, lifecycle segmentation, playbook automation, digital-success journeys, risk calibration, intervention measurement, and CS Operations dashboards.

## Phase 5 — Advanced intelligence

Add calibrated predictive signals, renewal/expansion readiness, account-level next-best-action proposals, feedback clustering, value attribution support, and cross-pack agent orchestration. Keep commercial and consequential decisions human-owned.

## MVP success criteria

The first production MVP should demonstrate: correct account isolation; trustworthy account context; measurable reduction in meeting-preparation time; useful risk explanations; evidence-backed success plans; no unauthorized customer contact; no commercial commitment generation; complete auditability; and zero production mutation capability.

# Research and Standards Notes

## Customer Success operating concepts

Current Customer Success platforms commonly organize work around Customer 360 context, health scorecards, success plans, playbooks, surveys/feedback, product adoption, organizational mapping, and renewal/expansion readiness. The pack uses those as capabilities without making any single vendor mandatory.

## Customer satisfaction standards

ISO 10004:2018 provides guidelines for monitoring and measuring customer satisfaction and was confirmed in 2023. ISO 10002:2018 provides guidelines for complaints handling, while ISO 10001:2018 covers customer satisfaction codes of conduct and ISO 10003:2018 addresses external dispute resolution. The pack uses these as quality-management references, not as legal advice or certification claims.

## Service management

Support SLAs and incident status remain owned by Support and SRE systems. Customer Success may consume those signals and translate customer impact but must not alter SLA targets, incident severity, or technical root cause.

## Health scoring

Health scorecards should use consistent, data-driven methods with visible components and weights. Composite scores are decision-support signals, not truth. The pack requires driver-level evidence, confidence, data freshness, and human-governed overrides.

## Commercial boundaries

Customer Success can support retention and growth through outcome evidence, adoption, risk mitigation, and relationship context. Pricing, discounts, quotes, contracts, renewal acceptance, credits, refunds, and revenue commitments remain with authorized commercial owners.

## Privacy

Customer Success often joins CRM, support, usage, communication, and commercial context. That breadth increases privacy and cross-tenant risk. The architecture therefore defaults to minimum-necessary retrieval, data minimization, purpose binding, short-lived access, account isolation, and payload-bound publication approvals.

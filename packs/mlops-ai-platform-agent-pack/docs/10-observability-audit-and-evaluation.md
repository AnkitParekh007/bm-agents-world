# Observability, Audit and Evaluation

The platform emits correlated metrics, logs and traces for control-plane services, pipelines, model/agent serving, retrieval, tool calls, evaluation, GPU scheduling, cost and policy decisions. Sensitive prompt/completion/document content is opt-in and redacted by default.

Audit events record actor, agent, project, environment, candidate, tool, request hash, policy result, approval, outcome and evidence references.

Platform evaluation covers reliability, developer experience, deployment safety, evaluation reproducibility, model lineage completeness, cost efficiency, capacity, tenant isolation and incident recovery. Quality claims about models remain owned by AI/ML Engineering; the platform validates that required evidence exists and is bound to the candidate.

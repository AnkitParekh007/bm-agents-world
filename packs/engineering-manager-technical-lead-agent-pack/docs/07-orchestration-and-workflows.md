# Orchestration and Workflows

Five stateful workflows are provided:

1. **Request to engineering plan** — converts product or technical demand into scoped workstreams, capacity scenarios, risks, and milestones.
2. **Sprint and delivery governance** — monitors flow, blockers, dependencies, quality, and forecast changes without micromanaging individuals.
3. **Technical decision and review** — produces technical options, architecture alignment, specialist reviews, and a human-owned decision record.
4. **Team health and capability** — prepares privacy-preserving team, coaching, growth, hiring-process, and onboarding artifacts.
5. **Incident and release coordination** — coordinates incident evidence, operational readiness, release recommendation, communication, and corrective actions.

Each workflow uses checkpoints, immutable evidence references, independent review for high risk, and payload-bound approvals for writes or execution.

# Implementation Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Read-only leadership assistant

Jira, Bitbucket, CI/CD, architecture, and aggregate metric reads; daily summary; delivery risks; technical review drafts.

## Phase 2 — Cross-pack orchestration

Delegate to Product, Solution Architecture, UX, developer, Database, QA, and DevOps packs; produce integrated plans and readiness reports.

## Phase 3 — Approval-controlled publication

Payload-bound Jira, Confluence, Teams, pull-request, incident, and release artifacts.

## Phase 4 — Restricted people workflows

Purpose-bound one-on-one preparation, coaching, growth, interview structure, and onboarding with separate storage and audits. No autonomous employment decisions.

## Phase 5 — Continuous improvement

Balanced DORA/SPACE/DevEx analysis, improvement experiments, forecast calibration, policy tuning, and independent safety evaluation.

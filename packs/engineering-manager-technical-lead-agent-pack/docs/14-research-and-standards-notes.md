# Research and Standards Notes

The pack uses balanced engineering-system measurement rather than individual activity surveillance. DORA informs software-delivery performance, SPACE provides multidimensional productivity dimensions, and DevEx focuses on feedback loops, cognitive load, and flow. These frameworks are decision aids and require local context, valid definitions, and safeguards against gaming.

Operational recommendations align with automation, evidence, structured change, reliability, and observability practices from cloud well-architected guidance. Agile artifacts are adapted to the organization's actual process rather than treated as mandatory ceremonies.

The pack does not reproduce proprietary standards or book content. Organization policy, labor law, employment practices, privacy obligations, and works-council or employee-representative requirements remain authoritative.

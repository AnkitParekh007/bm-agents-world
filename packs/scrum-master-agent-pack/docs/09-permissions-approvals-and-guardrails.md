# Permissions, Approvals, and Guardrails

## Safe autonomous actions

- Read approved team and delivery context
- Draft agendas, reports, questions, experiments, diagrams, and coaching plans
- Calculate configured team-level flow metrics
- Detect blocked work, dependencies, aging, and evidence gaps
- Prepare de-identified retrospective and team-health summaries

## Approval-controlled actions

- Schedule or modify Scrum events
- Create or update Jira comments, links, or impediment records
- Publish Confluence pages or Teams posts
- Launch team surveys
- Publish retrospective or team-health summaries
- Change workflow, WIP, or working-agreement policies
- Escalate organizational impediments externally

## Prohibited actions

- Assign work or owners to Sprint Backlog items without team decision
- Order the Product Backlog
- Estimate or commit work for Developers
- Approve releases or production changes
- Merge code or modify repositories
- Rank individuals or make employment decisions
- Read private messages or unrestricted people data
- Diagnose mental health or infer protected attributes
- Fabricate team feedback, metrics, decisions, or Scrum evidence

## Independent review

High-impact coaching interventions, team-health publications, systemic escalations, and workflow-policy changes require independent review and a human or team approval distinct from the authoring agent.

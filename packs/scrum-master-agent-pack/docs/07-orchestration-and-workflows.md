# Orchestration and Workflows

## State model

`INTAKE → AUTHORIZE → CONTEXT → ANALYZE → FACILITATE OR COACH → REVIEW → APPROVE → PUBLISH → OBSERVE → LEARN`

## Workflow catalogue

1. **Team launch and Scrum establishment** — establishes context, accountabilities, events, artifacts, working agreements, and initial improvement backlog.
2. **Sprint event facilitation** — prepares and facilitates Planning, Daily Scrum coaching, Review, and Retrospective while preserving accountable ownership.
3. **Impediment detection and resolution** — captures, classifies, routes, escalates, verifies, and learns from impediments.
4. **Flow health and delivery improvement** — analyzes team-level flow evidence and launches bounded improvement experiments.
5. **Retrospective to improvement execution** — turns de-identified retrospective learning into owned, measured experiments.

## Parallelism

Product, UX, architecture, engineering, QA, DevOps, quality, and stakeholder evidence may be collected in parallel. The supervisor converges findings and records disagreements rather than averaging them away.

## Approval gates

Approval is required for official Jira, Confluence, Teams, calendar, survey, retrospective-summary, workflow-policy, or escalation changes. Production and employment decisions are prohibited.

## Failure behavior

On missing evidence, expired approvals, privacy risk, ambiguous ownership, or connector failure, preserve the draft and evidence, mark the workflow blocked, and assign a human decision owner. Do not guess or publish.

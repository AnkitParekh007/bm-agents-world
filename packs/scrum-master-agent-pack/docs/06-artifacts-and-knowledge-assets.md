# Artifacts and Knowledge Assets

The registry defines **27 artifact types**. Each artifact records project, team, time window, sources, assumptions, authoring agent, review status, approvals, payload hash, and retention class.

## Core artifact families

### Scrum establishment

Team charter, working agreement, Scrum establishment assessment, and event calendar.

### Scrum events

Sprint Planning pack, Sprint Goal facilitation brief, Daily Scrum coaching guide, Sprint Review pack, Retrospective facilitation pack, and retrospective summary.

### Flow and delivery

Impediment register, organizational impediment brief, flow health report, Sprint health report, delivery risk register, and dependency map.

### Coaching and improvement

Team effectiveness assessment, privacy-preserving team health summary, coaching plan, conflict facilitation plan, improvement backlog, and improvement experiment.

### Governance and communication

Metrics definition catalog, daily summary, cross-team coordination pack, and handoff package.

## Knowledge hierarchy

1. Official Scrum Guide and approved organizational policies
2. Product Goal, Product Backlog, Sprint Goal, Definition of Done, and team working agreements
3. Approved product, architecture, quality, release, and incident evidence
4. Versioned team experiments and learning
5. Draft model analysis

Draft output never silently overrides approved artifacts.

# Observability, Audit, and Evaluation

## Runtime telemetry

Record run ID, project, team, purpose, agents invoked, connector calls, policy decisions, approval IDs, artifact hashes, errors, latency, and token/cost metadata. Never log raw secrets or unredacted sensitive people data.

## Quality evaluation

Evaluate:

- Scrum-accountability correctness
- Facilitation-plan clarity and inclusiveness
- Impediment classification and ownership quality
- Flow-metric calculation accuracy
- Metric interpretation and anti-gaming safeguards
- Privacy and redaction quality
- Evidence traceability
- Approval and publication compliance
- Team usefulness and reduction of manual coordination

## Outcome measures

Use team and system outcomes such as Sprint Goal effectiveness, blocked-time reduction, flow improvement, quality, stakeholder feedback, experiment completion, and recurring impediment reduction. Do not optimize for meeting count, ticket count, story points, or individual activity.

## Audit retention

Apply project policy. Sensitive coaching and retrospective evidence should have shorter retention and tighter access than ordinary delivery artifacts.

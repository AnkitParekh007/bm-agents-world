# Research and Standards Notes

## Scrum

The Scrum Guide remains the authoritative definition of Scrum. The pack uses the 2020 Guide's accountabilities, events, artifacts, commitments, values, and empirical foundations. The Scrum Master is modeled as accountable for establishing Scrum and for Scrum Team effectiveness, not as a project manager or people manager.

## Kanban practices with Scrum

The Kanban Guide for Scrum Teams is used as a complementary flow lens. Flow metrics and WIP policies enhance Scrum; they do not replace the Sprint, Product Goal, Sprint Goal, Increment, or accountabilities.

## Evidence-Based Management

The 2024 Evidence-Based Management Guide informs outcome-oriented improvement, hypotheses, experiments, and learning. It is not used to create individual performance scores.

## Privacy and employment safeguards

Team health, retrospective, and collaboration data require purpose limitation, minimum aggregation, de-identification, short retention, and human accountability. The agent cannot make consequential employment decisions.

## MCP and policy

MCP resources, prompts, and tools remain distinct. Sensitive tools sit behind OPA policy, short-lived capability leases, payload-bound approvals, and immutable audit records.

# Implementation Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Governance

Approve accountability boundaries, data classification, privacy, retention, escalation, and audit requirements.

## Phase 1 — Read-only assistant

Connect Jira, Confluence, Bitbucket, calendar, quality, and delivery analytics. Produce daily summaries, event packs, impediment drafts, and flow reports.

## Phase 2 — Team pilot

Pilot with one willing Scrum Team. Validate facilitation usefulness, metric accuracy, privacy, team trust, and reduction in manual preparation.

## Phase 3 — Approval-controlled publication

Enable calendar, Jira, Confluence, and Teams writes with payload hashes, expiry, replay protection, and audit.

## Phase 4 — Improvement experiments

Add experiment tracking, cross-team dependency analysis, organizational impediment escalation, and independent review.

## Phase 5 — Scale

Introduce shared templates, multi-team coordination, benchmarking against the team's own baseline, and pack-to-pack orchestration. Do not create individual rankings or cross-team velocity comparisons.

## Exit criteria

Proceed only when security, privacy, policy, accuracy, team consent, and operational support gates pass.

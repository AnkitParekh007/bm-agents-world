# Artifacts and Knowledge Assets

Artifacts are versioned, attributable, evidence-linked, redacted where necessary, and published only through the configured approval policy.

## data-product-brief
Purpose, users, outcomes, owners, sources, consumers, service levels, risks, and boundaries.

## source-system-inventory
Source ownership, schemas, extraction methods, volumes, freshness, quality, and constraints.

## source-to-target-mapping
Field-level source, transformation, target, rule, default, quality, and lineage mapping.

## data-contract
Versioned schema, semantics, ownership, service levels, compatibility, quality, and change policy.

## schema-registry-record
Approved schema identity, version, format, compatibility mode, subjects, and consumers.

## logical-data-model
Entities, relationships, grain, keys, business terms, and semantic definitions.

## physical-dataset-design
Tables, columns, types, partitioning, clustering, file format, retention, and access design.

## pipeline-architecture-design
Sources, stages, compute, storage, orchestration, quality, lineage, security, and operations.

## batch-job-specification
Inputs, transformations, outputs, partitions, dependencies, retries, controls, and success criteria.

## streaming-job-specification
Sources, sinks, offsets, watermarks, state, checkpoints, triggers, late data, and recovery.

## cdc-ingestion-specification
Keys, sequence, operation handling, deduplication, ordering, deletes, replay, and reconciliation.

## data-quality-specification
Rules, dimensions, thresholds, severity, quarantine, ownership, and enforcement behavior.

## data-quality-report
Run-bound rule results, invalid samples, trends, thresholds, actions, and evidence.

## reconciliation-report
Source and target totals, counts, hashes, exceptions, tolerance, and disposition.

## data-test-plan
Unit, contract, integration, quality, regression, scale, recovery, and acceptance tests.

## spark-performance-report
Plans, stages, scans, shuffles, skew, partitions, memory, runtime, cost, and recommendations.

## lineage-and-impact-map
Job, run, dataset, field, upstream, downstream, consumer, and change-impact relationships.

## pipeline-runbook
Operations, schedules, monitoring, failure modes, restart, replay, escalation, and recovery.

## backfill-plan
Scope, partitions, ordering, concurrency, validation, stop conditions, rollback, and reconciliation.

## data-migration-plan
Current and target state, compatibility, dual run, cutover, validation, and retirement.

## deployment-plan
Candidate identity, environments, sequencing, configuration, gates, monitoring, and owners.

## rollback-and-recovery-plan
Reversion, restore, checkpoint, replay, table snapshot, validation, and communication steps.

## data-incident-report
Impact, affected datasets, timeline, diagnosis, mitigation, correction, and follow-up.

## data-issue-defect
Reproduction, expected and actual data, affected partitions, evidence, severity, and ownership.

## release-evidence-bundle
Source revision, artifacts, contracts, tests, quality, performance, security, approvals, and hashes.

## cost-and-capacity-report
Current and forecast compute, storage, file, state, concurrency, and cost evidence.

## data-privacy-impact-assessment
Classification, purpose, minimization, access, retention, residency, masking, and deletion controls.

## production-action-request
Exact pipeline or data action, target, scope, payload hash, controls, approval, stop conditions, and verification.

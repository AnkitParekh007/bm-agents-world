# Observability, Audit, and Evaluation

## Pipeline observability

Track run identity, source revision, artifact, configuration, source and target datasets, partitions, input/output/rejected rows, freshness, duration, retries, quality, reconciliation, streaming lag, state, cost, and ownership.

## Audit evidence

Record authorization decisions, connector calls, bounded queries, artifacts, hashes, approvals, executions, stop conditions, verification, and publication. Do not log raw credentials or unrestricted data samples.

## Agent evaluation

Evaluate with historical stories, known data defects, schema changes, skewed datasets, late events, checkpoint recovery, backfills, and release exercises. Score:

- Requirement and contract coverage
- Transformation correctness
- Quality and reconciliation accuracy
- Performance recommendation validity
- Privacy and security compliance
- Production-boundary adherence
- Evidence completeness and provenance
- Appropriate uncertainty and escalation

## Anti-metrics

Do not reward lines of code, number of transformations, number of alerts, or volume of processed records without quality, reliability, cost, and outcome context.

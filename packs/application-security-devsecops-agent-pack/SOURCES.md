## Source-use policy

Organization policy, contracts, deployed versions, data classification, approved threat models, and actual scanner configuration are authoritative. External standards guide design and verification but do not grant permissions or replace accountable security decisions. Draft standards are labeled as drafts and must not be represented as final requirements.

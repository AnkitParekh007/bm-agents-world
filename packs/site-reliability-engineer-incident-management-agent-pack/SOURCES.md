
# Sources and Standards

The pack uses authoritative public sources as a baseline. Organization policy, contracts, architecture, risk owners, and approved runbooks remain controlling.

1. **NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 3 — Incident Response Recommendations and Considerations for Cybersecurity Risk Management**  
   Final publication, April 2025. Integrates incident response with CSF 2.0 risk management.

2. **Google Site Reliability Engineering — Service Level Objectives**  
   Guidance on user-focused SLOs, acceptable risk, and error budgets.

3. **Google SRE Workbook — Error Budget Policy**  
   Operational use of error budgets and policy thresholds.

4. **Google SRE Workbook — Postmortem Culture**  
   Factual, blameless post-incident learning and corrective action practices.

5. **OpenTelemetry Documentation and Specification**  
   Vendor-neutral traces, metrics, logs, context propagation, OTLP, and Collector concepts.

6. **Organization policies**  
   Incident severity, on-call, production access, service ownership, change management, security, privacy, data retention, and customer communication policies.

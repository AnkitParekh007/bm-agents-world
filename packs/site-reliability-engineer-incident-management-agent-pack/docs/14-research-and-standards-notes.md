
# Research and Standards Notes

## Reliability practice

Google's SRE guidance treats service level objectives and error budgets as a mechanism for balancing reliability and change. The pack therefore separates measurement, policy, and human decision authority.

## Incident response

NIST SP 800-61 Revision 3 is the current final incident-response publication and integrates incident-response considerations with cybersecurity risk management and CSF 2.0. Security incidents are escalated to the Application Security pack while the SRE pack coordinates service reliability and operational recovery.

## Observability

OpenTelemetry provides a vendor-neutral framework for traces, metrics, logs, and their correlation. The pack models observability as governed evidence rather than unrestricted access to raw production data.

## Post-incident practice

Post-incident reviews must be factual, evidence-linked, blameless, and oriented toward systemic learning. They must distinguish triggers, contributing conditions, uncertainty, and corrective actions.

## Organization-specific authority

These references inform the baseline but do not replace organization policy, architecture standards, contracts, regulatory obligations, service owners, incident command, or approved runbooks.

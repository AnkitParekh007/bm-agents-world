# Artifacts and Knowledge Assets

Artifacts are versioned evidence products. They must include source references, project/service scope, environment, timestamps, authorship, confidence, approvals, retention, and redaction state.

## `service-reliability-profile`

Service purpose, users, criticality, owners, dependencies, topology, risks, SLOs, runbooks, and recovery posture.

## `service-ownership-record`

Service owners, escalation paths, support hours, operational roles, and accountability boundaries.

## `sli-catalog`

Approved service-level indicators, user journeys, measurements, sources, filters, and data-quality constraints.

## `slo-specification`

SLI, target, window, exclusions, rationale, reporting, review cadence, and decision owner.

## `error-budget-policy`

Budget calculation, burn thresholds, release implications, exception path, and review responsibilities.

## `reliability-scorecard`

SLO performance, error budget, incident, alert, capacity, toil, and corrective-action summary.

## `alert-inventory`

Alert purpose, signal, threshold, severity, route, owner, runbook, suppression, and retirement state.

## `alert-quality-report`

Noise, actionability, duplicates, false positives, gaps, routing, and improvement recommendations.

## `operational-dashboard-specification`

Audience, decisions, panels, queries, units, thresholds, ownership, and data-quality notes.

## `operational-runbook`

Symptoms, prerequisites, diagnostics, safe actions, approvals, stop conditions, rollback, and verification.

## `oncall-readiness-brief`

Coverage, access, active risk, runbook, service, dependency, escalation, and handoff readiness.

## `incident-declaration-record`

Affected scope, severity, evidence, start time, roles, communication cadence, and uncertainty.

## `incident-command-brief`

Objectives, impact, roles, workstreams, hypotheses, actions, risks, decisions, and next update.

## `incident-timeline`

Immutable chronological events, alerts, changes, decisions, actions, communications, and evidence references.

## `diagnostic-plan`

Hypotheses, queries, checks, owners, safety limits, evidence requirements, and escalation criteria.

## `mitigation-options-report`

Options, expected effect, blast radius, prerequisites, stop conditions, rollback, evidence, and recommendation.

## `production-action-request`

Immutable exact action, target, reason, evidence, risk, approvals, expiry, rollback, and verification plan.

## `recovery-verification-report`

Technical, user, business, dependency, and observation-window evidence supporting recovery state.

## `incident-status-update`

Approved factual impact, investigation, mitigation, recovery, uncertainty, and next-update communication.

## `stakeholder-communication-plan`

Audiences, channels, owners, cadence, approval path, templates, and escalation rules.

## `post-incident-review`

Impact, timeline, detection, response, trigger, contributing factors, learning, and corrective actions.

## `corrective-action-register`

Actions, risk addressed, owner, priority, due date, status, evidence, and effectiveness review.

## `capacity-and-scalability-plan`

Demand scenarios, baselines, headroom, bottlenecks, thresholds, options, cost, and actions.

## `resilience-test-plan`

Hypothesis, scope, faults, safeguards, approvals, stop conditions, telemetry, recovery, and learning.

## `disaster-recovery-exercise-report`

Scenario, RTO/RPO, execution, recovery, reconciliation, gaps, evidence, and actions.

## `toil-and-automation-backlog`

Repetitive work, frequency, risk, effort, automation options, ownership, and outcome measures.

## `reliability-improvement-roadmap`

Prioritized reliability outcomes, initiatives, dependencies, milestones, measures, and review cadence.

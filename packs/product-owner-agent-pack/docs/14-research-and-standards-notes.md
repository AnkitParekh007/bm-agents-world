# Product Owner Agent — Research and Standards Notes

## Scrum Guide

The official Scrum Guide current version is November 2020. It makes the Product Owner accountable for maximizing product value and effective Product Backlog management, including developing and communicating the Product Goal, creating and clearly communicating Product Backlog items, ordering them, and ensuring transparency and understanding. The Product Owner may delegate work but remains accountable.

The Product Backlog is the single source of work undertaken by the Scrum Team. Developers create and own the Sprint Backlog plan; the Product Owner supports value and clarity but must not appropriate that accountability.

## Atlassian/Jira practice

Jira backlog capabilities support creating, ranking, refining, and planning product work. This pack keeps one transparent backlog, includes features, defects, technical work, research, and compliance, and treats ordering as a Product Owner decision rather than an agent-side optimization.

## Product Owner versus Product Manager

This pack distinguishes broader product strategy and market direction from team-level execution and backlog accountability. The exact organizational split is configurable; where one person holds both roles, the workflow still separates strategy evidence, backlog decisions, and independent review.

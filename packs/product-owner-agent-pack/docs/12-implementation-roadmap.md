# Product Owner Agent — Implementation Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Governance and inventory

Confirm accountable Product Owners, products, boards, workflows, data classes, decision rights, publication rules, and production prohibitions.

## Phase 1 — Read-only context MVP

Connect Jira/Confluence, repository context, designs, analytics definitions, support summaries, artifact storage, policy, and audit. Generate context, backlog-health, refinement, and daily summaries without external writes.

## Phase 2 — Drafting and readiness

Add story, acceptance, slicing, dependency, design/technical/QA readiness, Sprint Goal, and release-readiness artifacts. Validate all structured outputs.

## Phase 3 — Approval-controlled publication

Enable payload-bound Jira/Confluence/Teams publication with preview, named approver, expiry, idempotency, and immutable evidence.

## Phase 4 — Outcome feedback loop

Add approved analytics, feedback, UAT, release, and outcome review workflows. Track whether backlog decisions improve value, quality, predictability, and learning.

## Phase 5 — Cross-pack orchestration

Delegate to Product Manager, Business Analyst, UX, Solution Architect, Engineering Leadership, developer, QA, Database, and DevOps packs through typed handoff artifacts.

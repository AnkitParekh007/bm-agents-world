# Product Owner Agent — Permissions, Approvals, and Guardrails

## Autonomous low-risk work

- Read authorized context and approved evidence
- Draft Product Goal, backlog items, stories, acceptance criteria, plans, and reports
- Calculate configured prioritization scenarios
- Detect duplicates, aging, dependencies, and readiness gaps
- Create isolated artifacts and run deterministic validations

## Approval-controlled work

- Create, edit, transition, or reorder official Jira items
- Publish Confluence pages or Teams communications
- Contact customers or participants
- Launch research, surveys, betas, or experiments
- Record official Product Goal, scope, priority, release, or acceptance decisions

## Prohibited work

- Own or alter the Developers’ Sprint Backlog
- Dictate implementation or estimates
- Approve its own high-risk recommendation
- Make pricing, contractual, budget, legal, SLA, staffing, or guaranteed-date commitments
- Accept residual privacy, security, legal, operational, or commercial risk
- Merge code, deploy, change feature flags, mutate databases, infrastructure, IAM, networks, DNS, TLS, secrets, or production data
- Fabricate customer quotes, research, analytics, estimates, decisions, or acceptance evidence

## Approval contract

Every approval binds actor, authority, project, action, target, exact payload hash, evidence hash, known risks, expiration, and one-time execution identifier.

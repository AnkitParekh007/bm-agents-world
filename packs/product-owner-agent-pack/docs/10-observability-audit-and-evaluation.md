# Product Owner Agent — Observability, Audit, and Evaluation

## Operational telemetry

Track workflow duration, agent/tool calls, retries, policy denials, approval latency, connector failures, token/cost use, artifact generation, schema failures, and publication outcomes.

## Product Owner quality evaluation

Evaluate:

- Product Goal alignment and evidence quality
- Backlog transparency, ordering rationale, aging, duplication, and readiness
- Story clarity, vertical slicing, acceptance testability, and traceability
- Decision latency and number of unresolved questions entering delivery
- Dependency discovery and release-readiness accuracy
- Outcome-measure completeness and post-release learning
- Hallucination, unsupported commitment, and policy-violation rates

Do not use activity counts as individual employee performance scores.

## Audit event

Each event includes workflow, agent, requester, project, environment, data class, source references, tool, model/runtime version, policy decision, approval reference, input/output hashes, side effect, result, error, and timestamp.

# Product Owner Agent — Orchestration and Workflows

## Stateful execution model

Every workflow uses: **Intake → Scope authorization → Context → Evidence and gap analysis → Parallel specialist work → Synthesis → Independent review → Human decision/approval → Approved publication → Outcome and audit**.

## Included workflows

1. **Strategy to Team Backlog** — converts approved strategy and product outcomes into Product Goal and backlog options.
2. **Backlog Refinement and Readiness** — prepares ordered items, slicing, examples, acceptance, dependencies, and readiness evidence.
3. **Sprint Planning and Goal** — proposes a coherent Sprint Goal and ready candidates while preserving Developers’ ownership of Sprint Backlog selection and plan.
4. **Release Acceptance and Rollout** — consolidates quality, UX, technical, operational, support, and risk evidence for a human release decision.
5. **Feedback to Outcome Review** — combines analytics, support, research, quality, and business evidence to recommend backlog changes.

## Parallel delegation

UX, technical, QA, analytics, dependency, and risk assessments may run concurrently. The supervisor waits for mandatory evidence, records absent evidence explicitly, and cannot convert missing approval into presumed approval.

## Failure behavior

Stop privileged actions, preserve evidence, identify the unresolved decision or failed control, and route to the accountable human. Never bypass policy by switching tools or agents.

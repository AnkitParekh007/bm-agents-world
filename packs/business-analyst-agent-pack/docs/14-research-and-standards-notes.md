# Research and Standards Notes

The pack uses public standards as guidance while organization policy, product ownership, legal interpretation, and project conventions remain authoritative.

## Business analysis practice

IIBA BABOK Guide and The Business Analysis Standard inform planning, elicitation, requirements lifecycle, strategy, requirements analysis/design definition, and solution evaluation. This pack does not reproduce licensed standards content.

## Requirements engineering

ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148:2018 is the current confirmed requirements-engineering standard as of the pack date; an Edition 3 draft exists but is not treated as the approved baseline. The pack applies requirement quality, information-item, lifecycle, and traceability principles without claiming formal certification.

## Process modeling

OMG BPMN 2.0.2 is used as the process-modeling reference. The pack supports business-readable models and validation, but execution semantics depend on the organization’s selected BPM platform.

## Project and product integration

PMI Business Analysis for Practitioners and Requirements Management guidance inform collaboration with product, projects, and delivery. Jira and Confluence remain systems of record only where the organization designates them.

## Review cadence

Review standards, connector capabilities, data-protection policy, accessibility requirements, and project toolchains at least quarterly and before enabling new write capabilities.

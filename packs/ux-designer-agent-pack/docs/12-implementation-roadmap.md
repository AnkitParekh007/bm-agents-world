# Implementation Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Governance

Define UX ownership, research ethics/privacy process, Figma project boundaries, design-system owners, evidence retention, approval roles, and prohibited actions.

## Phase 1 — Read-only context MVP

Connect Jira/Confluence, Figma read, Bitbucket read, Storybook, design repository, artifact store, policy, and audit. Deliver story summaries, design briefs, flow reviews, accessibility annotations, and handoff drafts.

## Phase 2 — Isolated design workspace

Enable personal Figma drafts or approved branches, prototype generation, token checks, content linting, accessibility checks, and visual comparisons. Keep shared files and libraries human-controlled.

## Phase 3 — Research and validation

Add redacted research repository, consent-aware workflow, approved recruitment/recording adapters, usability analysis, privacy-thresholded analytics, and evidence-strength evaluation.

## Phase 4 — Handoff and design QA

Add Code Connect, Storybook, browser workers, implementation review, responsive/accessibility checks, Jira draft defects, and payload-bound publication approvals.

## Phase 5 — Shared design-system operations

After controls are proven, permit approved shared-file writes, branch merges, token/component proposals, and library releases through a separate privileged publisher.

## Success criteria

- No raw secrets or direct identifiers reach model context.
- Every design decision is traceable to evidence or labeled assumption.
- Accessibility and system-reuse checks are routine.
- Shared publication and participant-facing actions are approval-bound.
- Handoff and design QA reduce clarification and rework without bypassing designers, researchers, product owners, or engineers.

## Required artifact metadata

Every governed artifact should record: owner, scope, framework/version, systems, evidence references, source revision where relevant, generation timestamp, reviewer, decision status, approval record, retention class and superseded-by relationship.

## Knowledge assets

Approved sources include internal policies, control descriptions, architecture documents, evidence procedures, past audit findings, risk decisions, approved legal interpretations, customer commitments, regulatory registers, vendor evidence summaries and framework metadata. Licensed standards content should be referenced and mapped rather than copied wholesale.

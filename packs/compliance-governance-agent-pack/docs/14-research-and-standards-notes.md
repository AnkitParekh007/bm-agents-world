# Research and Standards Notes

The pack is designed as a governance operating system, not as legal advice or a substitute for auditors, counsel, DPOs or regulators.

Baseline frameworks and current references used during pack creation:

- NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 (2024), including the added emphasis on governance.
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022, with Amendment 1:2024 recorded in the standards registry.
- CIS Critical Security Controls v8.1.
- AICPA 2017 Trust Services Criteria with revised Points of Focus (2022) for SOC 2 contexts.
- EU GDPR Article 5 privacy and accountability principles.
- India Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and notified DPDP Rules 2025.
- EU AI Act, including transparency obligations applying from 2 August 2026 and updated high-risk timelines.
- NIST AI RMF 1.0 for AI-risk governance.

Because laws, standards, contracts and regulator guidance change, every formal assessment must resolve the applicable version and organization-approved legal interpretation at run time.

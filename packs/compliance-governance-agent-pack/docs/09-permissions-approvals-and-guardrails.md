# Permissions, Approvals and Guardrails

## Safe autonomous actions

- Read approved policies, controls, issues and framework metadata.
- Calculate mappings, evidence freshness and aggregate compliance metrics.
- Draft policies, findings, risk treatments, exception requests and reports.
- Run approved deterministic, read-only control tests.
- Create isolated artifacts and evidence manifests.

## Approval-controlled actions

- Publish or supersede official policies.
- Create/update official GRC controls, risks, findings or exceptions.
- Post official Jira, Confluence, Teams or customer-assurance content.
- Launch access-review campaigns, vendor assessments, surveys or attestations.
- Close material findings or approve control effectiveness.

## Human-only decisions

- Legal interpretation and legal privilege decisions.
- Regulatory notification decisions.
- Final risk acceptance or risk appetite changes.
- Auditor opinions or certification decisions.
- Contractual commitments.
- Employment, compensation or disciplinary decisions.
- Approval of the agent's own material exception or finding closure.

## Prohibited

No production mutation, credential retrieval, destructive evidence collection, unrestricted employee/customer surveillance, cross-tenant data mixing, fabricated evidence, fabricated approvals, silent framework substitution or bypass of retention/legal-hold rules.

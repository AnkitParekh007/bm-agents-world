# Compliance / Governance Agent Pack

A governed, reusable agent pack for enterprise compliance, risk, audit, privacy, AI governance and control assurance.

## Goals

- Normalize obligations into a reusable control library.
- Maintain evidence-backed scope, risk, findings and exceptions.
- Automate read-only evidence collection and deterministic control checks.
- Coordinate audits without fabricating assurance.
- Orchestrate remediation across engineering packs while preserving ownership.
- Keep legal interpretation, risk acceptance, certification and production change human-owned.

## Included profiles

- **NIST CSF 2.0** — Cybersecurity risk governance and outcomes; Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover.
- **ISO/IEC 27001:2022 + Amd 1:2024** — ISMS requirements, risk treatment, internal audit, management review, continual improvement.
- **ISO/IEC 27002:2022** — Information-security control guidance supporting ISO/IEC 27001 implementation.
- **CIS Controls v8.1** — Prioritized cybersecurity safeguards and implementation groups.
- **SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria** — Security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy criteria.
- **GDPR** — Privacy principles, accountability, data-subject rights, processor/controller obligations and DPIA governance.
- **India DPDP Act 2023 + Rules 2025** — Digital personal-data governance, notices, consent, rights, safeguards, breach and board processes.
- **PCI DSS v4.x** — Payment-card data controls when cardholder-data environments are in scope.
- **EU AI Act** — Risk-based AI governance, transparency, GPAI, provider/deployer obligations and high-risk-system governance.
- **NIST AI RMF 1.0** — AI governance, mapping, measurement and management of AI risks.
- **Internal Policies & Customer Commitments** — Organization-specific controls, contracts, security addenda, SLAs and policy obligations.
